Calculation details

These paired-review counts and rates are illustrative examples. Observed Recall counts Hard, Good and Easy as recalled, and excludes Again. The FSRS estimate is reconstructed retrievability immediately before those exact reviews. Differences use the exact observed fraction minus the estimate, in percentage points; displayed values are rounded. Missing counts and rates are unavailable, and long-dash bridges add no observations. The final 09/30–10/02 bucket is in progress as of 10/02, 2:44 AM.
